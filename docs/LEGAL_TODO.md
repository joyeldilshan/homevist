# Legal pages: what still has to be written

The Privacy Policy (`frontend/src/pages/Privacy.jsx`) and Terms of Service
(`frontend/src/pages/Terms.jsx`) are drafts. They used to show
`[PLACEHOLDER - legal review needed]` inline for every unknown. Those lines
were taken off the public pages, so the gaps are recorded here instead.

Nothing below may be guessed. Each item needs a fact about the business, and
both pages need review by someone qualified, before real patients use the
service. Several items carry regulatory weight for a health service.

## Privacy Policy

**1. Who we are**
- The data controller: registered name, company number and registered address.
- Contact address and email for questions about the policy.
- Data Protection Officer, if one is appointed.

**3. Why we use it, and our lawful basis**
- The lawful basis for processing personal data, and the separate Article 9
  condition relied on for health data.
- Whether the information is used for anything beyond delivering the service
  the patient asked for.

**4. Who we share it with**
- The laboratory that processes samples: name, role, and the agreement in place.
- Any other recipients, and whether any data leaves the UK.

**5. How long we keep it**
- Retention periods for accounts, booking records and test results.

**6. Your rights**
- How to make a rights request, and how long the response takes.

**7. How your information is stored**
- Hosting location, security measures and breach procedures.

**9. Changes to this policy**
- How users will be told about significant changes.

## Terms of Service

**1. Who provides this service**
- The provider: registered name, company number, registered address.
- Any registration with a healthcare regulator, and the registration number.
  Do not state a registration unless it exists and can be evidenced.

**2. What this service is — and is not**
- Whether any clinician reviews or interprets results as part of the service.

**3. Eligibility and service area**
- Minimum age, and arrangements for children or patients who lack capacity to
  consent.
- Medical conditions or circumstances where a sample cannot be collected at home.

**4. Booking, prices and payment**
- Live commercial prices (the app currently shows example prices; the page says so).
- When payment is taken, accepted methods, and whether VAT applies.

**5. Changing or cancelling an appointment**
- Notice periods, cancellation charges and refund terms.
- What happens if the phlebotomist cannot attend, or nobody is home.

**7. Results and reports**
- Expected turnaround times, and what happens if a result is delayed, unusable
  or needs repeating.
- How urgent or abnormal results are handled and escalated. This must be
  defined by a clinician.

**8. Liability**
- Limitations of liability.

**Complaints** (section removed until written)
- How to make a complaint, how long the response takes, and how to escalate it.

**Governing law** (section removed until written)
- Governing law and jurisdiction.
